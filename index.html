Foldaway

A calm arrow puzzle made of paper. Tap an arrow: if its way is clear, it slides off the sheet and folds into a paper bird.

Every ten sheets

Fold an origami zoo

Each sheet you solve adds one fold to the chapter's origami: a paper boat, a fox, a whale, an owl and more. The last sheet of every chapter is shaped like the figure you are about to finish.

Built to be fair

  • A tap between two arrows never costs a heart: nothing moves until you tap the one you mean.
  • Hold an arrow to see its path first. Holding never costs a heart.
  • Leave in the middle of a sheet and pick up exactly where you left off.
  • Hearts only count inside a sheet. Run out and you can watch a short video for one more heart, or start the sheet over at once, for free.
  • Clear ad rules: no full-screen ad at launch or in the middle of a sheet, none in your first seven sheets, then at most one every three sheets. A small banner sits below the board.

Foldaway plays offline and keeps your progress on your phone. It is available in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese, and you can switch the language in Settings.
